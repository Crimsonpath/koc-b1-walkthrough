How to walk

On a phone

iPhone or Android · opens in its own tab

  1. Hold it sideways — For the widest view
  2. Tap to start — Then enter the password
  3. Left stick — Walk
  4. Right stick — Look around

On a laptop

Windows or Mac · mouse or trackpad

  1. Click the view — So it takes your keys
  2. WASD — Walk
  3. Drag — Look around

Press Esc to leave full screen.

Good to know

  • Password You'll be asked for the password you received separately.

  • iPhone iPhone doesn't allow full screen inside a web page, so the walkthrough opens in its own tab, where it has the whole window.

  • Idle sessions The session closes after a while without movement, or when you switch apps. Open the walkthrough again to reconnect.

  • Connection Works best on Wi-Fi or a strong 4G/5G signal.
